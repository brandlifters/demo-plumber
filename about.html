Our Story

Started in a Pickup Truck.
Built on Reputation.

FlowRight Plumbing was founded in 2008 by Marcus Rivera, a Denver native who spent a decade apprenticing under some of the city's best master plumbers before going out on his own.

He started with one truck, a solid reputation, and a simple philosophy: do the job right, charge a fair price, and treat every customer's home like your own. Word spread quickly across Lakewood, then Aurora, and eventually across the entire metro area.

Today, FlowRight runs a team of eight licensed technicians and a fleet of five fully stocked service vehicles. The business has grown, but the values haven't changed. We're still the same local company we were on day one — just with more trucks.

  • Founded by a licensed master plumber with 25+ years of experience
  • 8 licensed, background-checked technicians on our team
  • Serving 12 cities across the Denver Metro Area
  • Over 2,400 completed jobs and counting

How We Work

Our Core Values

Honest Work, Always

We diagnose the actual problem and recommend only what you truly need. No upselling, no unnecessary repairs, no inflated quotes.

Respect for Your Time

We show up in the window we promise. If something changes, we call ahead — never leave you guessing when your plumber is arriving.

Fair, Transparent Pricing

Every job gets a flat-rate quote before we touch anything. No hidden fees, no surprise overages — the number you're quoted is the number you pay.

Treating Your Home With Care

We wear shoe covers, lay down drop cloths, and clean up after ourselves every single time. Your space should look better when we leave than when we arrived.

Quality That Lasts

We use professional-grade materials and follow all Colorado code requirements. Our repairs are built to last — not just to pass the first inspection.

Community First

We live and work in Denver. We're invested in the community we serve — and we show it by giving back through local partnerships and discounts for seniors and veterans.
