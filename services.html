24/7 Emergency

Emergency Plumbing

When a pipe bursts, a drain backs up, or water starts flooding your home, every minute matters. Our licensed emergency plumbers are on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including holidays. Average dispatch time is under 45 minutes anywhere in the Denver Metro Area.

  • Burst pipe containment & repair
  • Sewage backups & overflow
  • Water shut-off & flood control
  • Gas line emergency shutoffs
  • ~45 min avg. response time

Drain Cleaning & Unclogging

Slow or completely blocked drains are one of the most common plumbing problems — and one of the most disruptive. We use professional-grade snake and hydro-jet equipment to clear blockages thoroughly and prevent them from coming back quickly.

  • Kitchen & bathroom drain clearing
  • Main sewer line cleaning
  • Hydro-jetting for stubborn clogs
  • Camera inspection available
  • Root intrusion removal

Leak Detection & Repair

Even small leaks cause major damage if left unchecked — rotting subfloors, mold growth, and sky-high water bills. Our technicians use non-invasive detection tools to pinpoint leaks precisely before cutting into walls, saving you time and money.

  • Behind-wall & under-slab detection
  • Supply & drain line repairs
  • Slab leak repair & rerouting
  • Pressure testing
  • Joint, coupling & fitting replacement

Water Heater Services

Cold showers and inconsistent hot water are signs your water heater needs attention. We service, repair, and replace all major brands of tank and tankless water heaters. Most standard replacements are completed the same day.

  • Tank water heater repair & replacement
  • Tankless water heater installation
  • Anode rod & element replacement
  • Thermostat & pilot light repair
  • Water heater flushing & maintenance

Toilet, Sink & Faucet Repair

Dripping faucets, running toilets, and faulty shut-off valves waste water and money around the clock. We repair or replace all types of residential fixtures quickly and cleanly — with minimal disruption to your day.

  • Running & leaking toilet repair
  • Faucet & cartridge replacement
  • Sink & vanity installation
  • Shut-off valve replacement
  • Garbage disposal install & repair

Pipe Repair & Installation

Corroded, cracked, or aging pipes can cause widespread water damage if ignored. Whether you need a single section repaired or a full home repipe, our team handles it efficiently and to Colorado plumbing code.

  • Copper, PEX & CPVC piping
  • Full home repiping
  • Frozen pipe thaw & prevention
  • New construction rough-in
  • Outdoor & irrigation line work
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you respond to a plumbing emergency?
Our average emergency response time is approximately 45 minutes anywhere in the Denver Metro Area. We keep fully stocked service vehicles on the road so our technicians can handle most repairs on the first visit without needing to source parts.
Are your plumbers licensed and insured?
Yes — every technician holds a valid Colorado Journeyman or Master Plumber license. We carry full general liability insurance and are bonded. Our license number is CO-PLB-47821 and is available for verification upon request.
Do you offer free estimates?
We offer free estimates on most planned work such as installations, repiping, and fixture replacements. For diagnostic or emergency callouts, a standard trip charge applies, which is credited toward the repair cost if you proceed with us.
What areas do you serve?
We serve the full Denver Metro Area including Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, Englewood, Littleton, Arvada, Westminster, Thornton, Centennial, Highlands Ranch, Parker, and Castle Rock. If you're unsure whether we cover your address, give us a call.
Do you work on weekends and holidays?
Yes. Our standard hours are Monday–Friday 7 AM–7 PM and Saturday 8 AM–5 PM. Emergency service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including all major holidays.
How does your pricing work?
We use flat-rate pricing — we quote the complete job price before we start work, and that number doesn't change unless the scope of the job changes (which we'll always discuss with you first). We never charge surprise hourly overages.
Do you handle commercial plumbing jobs?
We specialize in residential plumbing but also handle light commercial work for small offices, retail spaces, and multi-unit buildings. Contact us to discuss your specific needs and we'll let you know if it's within our scope.
